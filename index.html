Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Siddhashila EEVVA, Punawale, Pune

Where is Siddhashila EEVVA located in Pune? +

Siddhashila EEVVA is strategically situated on an expansive 30-meter wide DP Road in Punawale, Pimpri-Chinchwad (PCMC), West Pune. It is located minutes from the Hinjewadi IT Park (Phase 1 & 2), Mumbai-Pune Expressway, Bhumkar Chowk, and the upcoming Pune Metro line.

What are the flat configurations and carpet areas at EEVVA? +

The project offers premium 2 BHK and 3 BHK luxury configurations: 2 BHK Classic with carpet areas of 801 – 806 sq.ft., 3 BHK Premia with 969 – 973 sq.ft., and 3 BHK Luxuria with 1102 sq.ft. carpet area.

What is the starting price for flats in Siddhashila EEVVA Punawale? +

The 2 BHK luxury residences at Siddhashila EEVVA start from ₹83 Lakhs onwards*. Detailed pricing breakdowns, floor-rise details, and early bird launch discounts can be requested via our official enquiry form.

What makes the rhombus geometry of Siddhashila EEVVA unique? +

Inspired by the Sri Yantra sacred geometry, the master layout arranges towers in a deliberate rhombus pattern. This design ensures maximum spacing between residential towers, superior natural airflow & daylight from multiple directions, minimal overlooking between balconies, only 2 homes per wing, and just 1 shared wall for total peace and privacy.

Is Siddhashila EEVVA MahaRERA registered? +

Yes, Siddhashila EEVVA is fully registered with the Maharashtra Real Estate Regulatory Authority under MahaRERA Registration No. PM1260002503079. All project details, sanctioned building plans, and approvals are verified on the official MahaRERA portal at maharerait.maharashtra.gov.in.

Who is the developer behind EEVVA, and what is their track record? +

Siddhashila EEVVA is developed by Siddhashila Group (in association with Adi Group), celebrated makers of landmark Pune developments including Eela and Eira. The group holds a renowned reputation for on-time delivery, structural excellence, and premium lifestyle communities across Pune.

Why is Siddhashila EEVVA considered a top real estate investment in Pune? +

Punawale is West Pune's highest capital appreciation corridor due to its direct linkage with Hinjewadi Phase 1 & 2, Wakad, and the Mumbai-Pune Expressway. With an expansive 30m DP road frontage, 3.5 acres land parcel, 60+ curated amenities across 46,000 sq.ft., and a dedicated 9-storey boutique commercial hub, EEVVA offers exceptional rental yields and sustained capital growth.
